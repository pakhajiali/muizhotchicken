What is Nasi Kandar kuah banjir?

Kuah banjir – meaning 'flooded gravy' – is a style of serving Nasi Kandar where multiple curries (fish curry, beef gravy, and sweet soy sauce) are mixed together over steamed white rice. It creates a rich, complex flavour profile that's unique to each stall. We've perfected this technique using a 15-spice blend inspired by Penang's heritage recipes.
