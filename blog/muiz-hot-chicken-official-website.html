🏆 About Muiz Hot Chicken

Muiz Hot Chicken was founded in 2013 by Muhamad Muizzuddin Remle in Parit Raja, Johor. What started as a humble roadside kiosk earning RM20 a day has grown into a nationwide sensation with 180+ kiosks, five restaurant branches, and RM45 million in total sales (2025).

The brand is built on a family recipe – chicken marinated for 12 hours in a blend of 12 local herbs and spices, then double-coated for a crispy crunch that stays crispy even during delivery. This unique recipe has won the hearts of Malaysians and earned the brand a loyal following.

Muiz Hot Chicken is 100% JAKIM Halal certified and holds MeSTI certification from the Ministry of Health Malaysia. The brand is also recognised by major media outlets, including Berita Harian, which featured its inspiring success story.
