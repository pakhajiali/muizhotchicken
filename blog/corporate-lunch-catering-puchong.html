Halal Certification: What to Verify Before You Book

Not all "halal" caterers are equal. In Malaysia, the gold standard is JAKIM Halal certification — issued by the Department of Islamic Development Malaysia. A restaurant may claim to be "Muslim-owned" or "pork-free" without holding actual JAKIM certification.

When evaluating a corporate caterer in Puchong, ask for:

  1. JAKIM Halal certificate number. Verify it on the official Halal Malaysia Portal.
  2. KKM MeSTI certification. This confirms the kitchen meets Ministry of Health food safety standards (Makanan Selamat Tanggungjawab Industri).
  3. Ingredient sourcing. Ask where their chicken, beef, and seafood come from. Reputable caterers will answer without hesitation.
  4. Kitchen inspection reports. A serious caterer will have recent inspection documentation available.
