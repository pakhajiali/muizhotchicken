Why Goodyear Court 6 Residents Love Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken

They don't just eat here — they treat it like an extension of their own kitchen. Five reasons the Goodyear Court 6 community made it their default dinner spot.

3 October 2026 5 min read Goodyear Court 6, USJ 8
Some restaurants are destinations. Others become part of the neighbourhood. Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken sits right inside Goodyear Court 6 at Subang Perdana Phase 3 — and for the residents who live in the blocks around it, it has quietly become the answer to the 6:30 PM question: "What are we eating tonight?"
2 min Walk from most blocks

The Five Reasons

From a two-minute walk to a family dinner under RM50 — here's why the neighbourhood keeps coming back.

Reason 01

It's a Two-Minute Walk, Not a Drive

Zero parking headaches

Goodyear Court 6 is a residential neighbourhood. Residents don't want to fight for parking at a mall or sit in USJ traffic just to feed their families. Having a proper sit-down restaurant inside the neighbourhood itself — at J2-00-04, Subang Perdana Phase 3 — changes the whole equation.

Dinner in under 10 minutes. Walk over, grab a Nasi Kandar or a Happy Box, and be back home before the rice goes cold.
No traffic, no toll, no stress. For families with young kids, the difference between "let's go out for dinner" and "let's just eat at home" is often just the drive.
Reason 02

The Parcel Counter Made It a Daily Habit

Shopee SPX + TikTok Shop Yoyi

Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is an official Shopee SPX Self-Collection Point and a TikTok Shop Yoyi Station. That means residents are already walking into the restaurant multiple times a week to pick up parcels.

The result is a habit loop:

1
You walk over after work to collect a Shopee parcel.
2
You smell the fresh fried chicken and see the Nasi Kandar station.
3
You tapau dinner for the family on your way out.
4
Next parcel arrives → you already know what you're having for dinner.
Why residents love it: it's the only place in the neighbourhood where picking up a parcel and picking up dinner happen in the same 90 seconds.
Reason 03

It Feeds a Whole Family for Under RM50

Inflation-proof pricing

Feeding a family of four outside the house in 2026 is expensive. But at Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken, a family dinner doesn't have to break the budget.

Budget Solo Meal

A 2-piece Muiz Hot Chicken box, or a Nasi Kandar Biasa — enough to fill one person up after a long day.

RM6 – RM10

Family Sharing Box

An Original Happy Box (5 pieces of Muiz Hot Chicken) plus rice and sides — feeds a family of 4 comfortably.

Under RM50
Why residents love it: it's one of the few places left in USJ 8 where a family can eat properly for the price of one fast-food combo meal elsewhere.
Reason 04

You Never Get Bored of the Menu

Nasi Kandar, Thai, Chicken & more

Most neighbourhood restaurants serve one thing well and that's it. The problem? After a few weeks, you've eaten everything twice. Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken solves this by running multiple kitchens under one roof.

Nasi Kandar with rich curries, fried chicken and all the classic lauk you'd expect from a proper Penang-style stall.
Thai Tomyam and other Thai-inspired dishes for when you want something hot, sour and different.
Muiz Hot Chicken — the signature fried chicken that built the brand — in Regular Box, Happy Box and Korean Cheese variants.
ZUS Coffee combos for the days you just want something quick with a proper cup of coffee.
Why residents love it: Nasi Kandar on Monday, Tomyam on Tuesday, fried chicken on Wednesday — the menu rotates naturally because the kitchen can actually deliver on all of it.
Reason 05

Halal Certified, and Delivery-Ready When You're Not

JAKIM Halal • MeSTI

USJ 8 has a large Muslim community, and "Muslim-owned" isn't the same as "JAKIM Halal certified". Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken holds both JAKIM Halal certification and KKM MeSTI certification — so residents don't have to second-guess what they're feeding their families.

JAKIM Halal certified. The gold standard in Malaysia — verifiable, not just a claim on the signboard.
KKM MeSTI certified. Confirms the kitchen meets Ministry of Health food safety standards — important for families with young children.
Available on Grab Dine Out & Foodpanda. For rainy nights, sick days, or when you genuinely can't leave the house, the same food arrives at your door.
Why residents love it: certified halal, food-safe, and it comes to you — no compromises, no second-guessing.
