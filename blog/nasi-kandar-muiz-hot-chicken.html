Nasi Kandar Muiz Hot Chicken
Nasi Putih, Kuah Kari Pekat & Heaven

August 1, 2026 4 min read Nasi Kandar, Muiz Hot Chicken, Kuah Kari Pekat 5★ Best Seller

Nasi Kandar Muiz Hot Chicken is proof that greatness lies in simplicity. We don't overcomplicate things. It is just three perfect elements on a plate: fluffy steamed white rice (Nasi Putih), a generous drenching of our thick, aromatic curry sauce (Kuah Kari Pekat), and our legendary crispy Muiz Hot Chicken. That's it. And it tastes absolutely heavenly.

✨ Nasi Putih + Kuah Kari Pekat + Muiz Hot Chicken = The Perfect Bite. ✨

1. Nasi Putih – The Fluffy Canvas

Every great Nasi Kandar starts with the rice. Our Nasi Putih is steamed to perfection – fluffy, fragrant, and slightly warm. It acts as the perfect canvas to soak up all the rich, spicy goodness of our curry. Each grain is separate, light, and ready to carry the bold flavours of the dish.

2. Kuah Kari Pekat – The Soul of the Dish

This is where the magic happens. Our Kuah Kari Pekat (thick curry sauce) is the soul of the dish. Slow-cooked for hours with a secret blend of 15 herbs and spices, it develops a deep, rich, and velvety texture that clings to every grain of rice. It's aromatic, mildly spicy, and packed with umami. This is not a watery gravy; this is a curry you can sink your teeth into.

We ladle it generously over the Nasi Putih – no skimping, because we know the kuah banjir (flooded gravy) style is the only way to eat it.

3. Muiz Hot Chicken – The Crispy Crown

Last but certainly not least, the star of the show: our Muiz Hot Chicken. Marinated for 12 hours in a proprietary spice blend, then double-coated and fried until golden, crispy, and incredibly juicy on the inside. Placed right next to the curry-drenched rice, it offers the perfect textural contrast – crunchy exterior, tender meat, and a subtle heat that harmonizes beautifully with the rich curry.

Why It Tastes Like Heaven

It's the synergy of textures and flavours. You take a spoonful of Nasi Putih soaked in Kuah Kari Pekat, and add a piece of that crispy Muiz Hot Chicken. The creamy, spicy curry coats your palate, the fluffy rice provides comfort, and the crispy chicken gives a satisfying, savoury crunch. It's simple, bold, and utterly addictive.
