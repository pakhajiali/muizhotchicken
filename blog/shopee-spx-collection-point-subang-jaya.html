Great news for Shopee shoppers in Subang Jaya! Restoran Pak Haji Ali is now an official Shopee SPX self‑collection point, operated by CollectCo. This means you can pick up your Shopee parcels right here, while also enjoying our famous Muiz Hot Chicken and other delicious dishes.

How it works: After placing your order on Shopee, select this location as your pickup point. You'll receive a collection code in the Shopee app. Just show that code at our counter, and we'll hand you your parcel – it's that simple!

  • Long opening hours – Daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, so you can pick up after work or class.
  • Free parking – No hassle, just park and collect.
