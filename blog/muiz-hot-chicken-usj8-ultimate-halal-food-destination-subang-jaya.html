The Signature Muiz Hot Chicken Experience

What sets Muiz Hot Chicken apart is the 12-hour marination in a secret blend of 12 local herbs and spices, followed by a double-coating technique that delivers a light, crispy crunch with every bite. Unlike other fried chicken chains, our chicken stays crispy even during delivery – a testament to our quality.

Pair it with our signature cheese sauce or the Korean spicy glaze, and you'll understand why customers drive from as far as Puchong and Shah Alam just to dine at our USJ 8 outlet.

Authentic Nasi Kandar – Kuah Banjir Style

We don't just serve fried chicken. Our Nasi Kandar Kuah Banjir is a love letter to Penang's heritage – a plate of fluffy steamed rice flooded with a symphony of curries: fish curry, beef gravy, and a sweet soy sauce blend. We use a 15-spice recipe that has been perfected over generations, giving each spoonful a complex, layered flavour profile that keeps customers coming back.

For the ultimate experience, order the Nasi Kandar with Muiz Hot Chicken – the crispy chicken soaks up the curries beautifully, creating a harmony of textures and tastes that is simply unmatched.
