Muiz Hot Chicken – From RM20 a Day to RM45 Million

The inspiring success story of Muhamad Muizzuddin Remle – from a roadside kiosk in Parit Raja, Johor, to Malaysia's fastest-growing halal crispy chicken franchise with 180+ outlets and RM45 million in sales.

Muiz Hot Chicken is Malaysia's fastest-growing halal crispy chicken franchise – and its success story is nothing short of inspiring. What started as a roadside kiosk in Parit Raja, Johor, earning just RM20 a day in profit has grown into a nationwide phenomenon with 180+ kiosks, five restaurant branches, and RM45 million in total sales in 2025.

The journey of Muhamad Muizzuddin Remle, the founder of Muiz Hot Chicken, is a testament to resilience, hard work, and the power of a dream. Today, his brand is a household name in Malaysia – and we're proud to be part of the Muiz Hot Chicken family at Restoran Pak Haji Ali in Subang Jaya USJ 8.

👨‍🍳 The Founder's Story – Muhamad Muizzuddin Remle

Muhamad Muizzuddin Remle started his business at the age of 20 on 18 February 2013 – just three days after his 20th birthday. He set up a small kiosk by the roadside in Parit Raja, Johor, selling his family's secret recipe for crispy fried chicken.

His first day of business was a humbling experience – he earned only RM20 in profit. But that moment became the turning point. Instead of giving up, he used that experience as fuel to work harder, refine his recipe, and build a business that would one day become a Malaysian success story.

"Bermula daripada kiosk tepi jalan dengan meraih untung RM20 sehari pada hari pertama berniaga, detik sukar itu menjadi pemangkin kejayaan pengasas Muiz Hot Chicken untuk membangunkan perniagaan yang kini memiliki lebih 180 kiosk dan lima cawangan restoran dengan jumlah jualan mencecah sekitar RM45 juta pada 2025."
— Berita Harian, July 2026

The recipe for Muiz Hot Chicken was handed down from his aunt, and Muizzuddin perfected it over the years. Today, the brand is known for its 12-hour marinade and double-coated crunch – a combination that has won the hearts of Malaysians nationwide.
