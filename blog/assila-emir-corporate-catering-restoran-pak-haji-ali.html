Corporate Catering: Extending Our Restaurant Beyond Its Premises

Corporate catering represents an important opportunity for food and beverage businesses to serve customers outside their regular dine-in operations.

Unlike a conventional restaurant visit, catering involves preparing food for a specific group, coordinating quantities, meeting agreed requirements, and arranging delivery or collection where applicable.

For businesses, educational institutions, events, and organised gatherings, reliable catering can simplify meal arrangements and provide a practical solution for feeding groups of people.

At Restoran Pak Haji Ali, we see catering as a complementary part of our business model.

Our objective is to make our food offerings accessible to customers who need meals for organised gatherings, meetings, and other group occasions.

Every catering enquiry also provides an opportunity to understand customer requirements, improve our operational processes, and develop relationships beyond our immediate neighbourhood.

Yes. Restoran Pak Haji Ali USJ 8 provides corporate and group catering for offices, events, educational institutions and organised gatherings in Subang Jaya and the surrounding Klang Valley area. Enquiries can be made via WhatsApp at +60 17-908 1447.
Who is Assila Emir?

Catering options include Nasi Kandar sets, Muiz Hot Chicken boxes (Original, Cheese, Korean Spicy, Korean Cheese and Happy Box sharing platters), Malaysian rice meals, mee, bakso and beverage add-ons from ZUS Ngupi. Packages can be tailored to group size and requirements.
