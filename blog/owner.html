Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is the result of a powerful partnership between two brothers – Ali (Master in Chemical Engineering, UCTATi) and Nalif (Degree in Mechanical Engineering, UMP) – who grew up together and now build a business together. Their complementary skills make them a formidable team in the competitive Subang Jaya food scene.

Ali brings the vision – he excels in generating bold ideas, building software solutions, and streamlining operations. He is the creator of Mobile Muslim and the full‑stack developer who built this entire restaurant website from scratch. His technical foundation was built at UCTATi, where he earned his Master in Chemical Engineering Technology.

Nalif brings the strategy – he leads marketing efforts, brand building, and customer engagement, while also assisting with daily operations. Nalif honed his analytical and strategic skills at UMP (University Malaysia Pahang), where he earned his Degree in Mechanical Engineering.

🏗️ Building a Legacy in Subang Jaya

🍗

Shared Vision

Ali (Master in Chemical Engineering, UCTATi) and Nalif (Degree in Mechanical Engineering, UMP) envisioned a restaurant that would serve as a pillar of the Subang Jaya community – a place where families, students, and professionals could enjoy authentic halal food in a welcoming environment. Located at Goodyear Court 6, USJ 8.

🛡️

Commitment to Halal Integrity

The restaurant holds both JAKIM Halal and MeSTI certifications, ensuring every dish is prepared with complete peace of mind. "It's not just about the food; it's about trust," they emphasize.

🤝

A Brotherly Partnership

Ali (UCTATi, Chemical Engineering) focuses on the technical backbone – from building the website to managing kitchen operations and creating the Mobile Muslim app. Nalif (UMP, Mechanical Engineering) focuses on the brand's voice – from marketing campaigns to social media engagement and customer relations. Together, they cover every angle of the business.

🔮

Future Vision

Looking ahead, the brothers plan to expand the restaurant's presence through more catering services for corporate events, weddings, and private parties. "We want to be the go-to choice for halal catering in Subang Jaya," they say.

📖 Mobile Muslim – Quran & Qibla App

📱

Created & Developed by Ali (UCTATi)

Mobile Muslim features a complete Quran with full Jawi script support and an accurate Qibla direction compass. The app reflects Ali's commitment to serving the Muslim community – combining traditional Islamic values with modern technology.
