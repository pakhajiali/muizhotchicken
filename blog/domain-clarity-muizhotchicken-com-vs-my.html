muizhotchicken.com vs muizhotchicken.com.my

August 1, 2026 3 min read Domain, Brand, Flagship Outlet

We understand that having two similar domain names can be confusing. This page is here to clearly explain the difference between muizhotchicken.com and muizhotchicken.com.my – and which one you should use for the flagship outlet experience.

Who Owns Each Domain?

🔴 muizhotchicken.com

Owned by: Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken (the flagship outlet)

Purpose: Direct website for the Subang Jaya (USJ 8) flagship outlet – menu, promotions, location, and orders.

Audience: Customers who want to visit or order from the original flagship outlet.

🔵 muizhotchicken.com.my

Owned by: Muiz Hot Chicken Franchisor (corporate / headquarters)

Purpose: Corporate information, franchise opportunities, brand history, and general Muiz Hot Chicken brand communications.

Audience: Potential franchisees, investors, and media looking for corporate details.

Both domains are legitimate and represent different aspects of the Muiz Hot Chicken ecosystem. They complement each other – one focuses on the consumer experience at the flagship outlet, the other on the broader brand and business.

Which One Should You Use?

  • For dining, takeaway, or delivery from the flagship outlet – visit muizhotchicken.com. This is where you will find our full menu, location details, and direct ordering options.
  • For franchise inquiries or corporate information – visit muizhotchicken.com.my. That site is managed by the franchisor and provides business-related resources.

Why the .com is the Flagship Outlet's Official Site

Restoran Pak Haji Ali is the first and flagship outlet of the Muiz Hot Chicken brand. We have operated at our current location in Subang Jaya (USJ 8) since 2026, and we continue to serve as the benchmark for quality and authenticity. Our website, muizhotchicken.com, reflects our direct connection to the community and our dedication to providing the best Muiz Hot Chicken experience.

When you visit muizhotchicken.com, you are interacting directly with our outlet team – the same people who cook your food, take your orders, and welcome you to our restaurant. It is your one-stop portal for everything related to the flagship outlet.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is muizhotchicken.com an official site? – Yes, it is the official site of Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken, the flagship outlet.
  • Does the franchisor approve of muizhotchicken.com? – Absolutely. We operate under the brand's guidelines and maintain a strong partnership with the franchisor.
  • Can I order from muizhotchicken.com? – Yes, you can find our menu and order via WhatsApp or delivery platforms directly from our site.

Final Word

We hope this clarifies the domain situation. Both muizhotchicken.com and muizhotchicken.com.my serve important roles – one for the flagship outlet experience, the other for the franchise brand. Choose the one that matches your need, and we look forward to serving you at muizhotchicken.com – the home of the original Muiz Hot Chicken in Subang Jaya.
