Standard Operating Procedure (Muiz)

Preparation, breading, frying and completion — the full chicken station procedure, step by step.

Deep fryer station 13 min cycle Kitchen staff
This procedure covers the complete fried chicken workflow at Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken - Subang Jaya (USJ 8). Follow it in order: prepare → bread → fry → complete. Do not skip the preparation check — the oil and temperature must be correct before any chicken goes in.
17 L Oil volume
180°C Frying temperature
13:00 Regular chicken timer
20 pcs Standard batch

1. Preparation

Get the chicken and the oil right before anything else.

Step 01

Set Up the Station

Tap each item when done
Check the chicken. Ensure the chicken is fully defrosted and smells fresh. Do not use chicken that is still frozen or off-smelling.
Prep the oil. Fill the deep fryer with 17 litres of oil, keeping the level 1 cm below the max line.
Set the temperature. Turn on the deep fryer and set it to 180°C. Wait until it reaches this temperature.
Confirm the heat indicator. The "small fire" indicator means the oil has reached 180°C and will maintain that heat.
Do not start frying until the indicator shows the oil is at 180°C. Breading the chicken while the oil heats up is fine — putting chicken into cold oil is not.

2. Breading

Do this while waiting for the oil to reach 180°C.

Step 02

Flour → Water → Flour

Regular 20 pcs • Tenders 3 pcs

The breading sequence is the same for regular chicken and chicken tenders. Only the quantity changes.

1
First flour coat. Coat the chicken pieces in flour and mix well.
2
Water dip. Immerse the pieces in water.
3
Second flour coat. Coat them in flour a second time and mix again.
Regular chicken: 20 pieces per batch, following flour → water → flour.
Chicken tenders: 3 pieces per batch, following the exact same steps — flour → water → flour.

3. Cooking & Frying

Two approved methods. Use Method A unless the situation calls for Method B.

Method A

Staggered Cooking (Standard)

Default method

First, separate the chicken thigh and drumsticks from the breast, ribs and wings. Then set the timer to 13 minutes and follow the timeline below.

13:00
Timer starts — thighs & drumsticks in Put the thighs and drumsticks into the deep fryer and start the timer.
11:00
2 minutes later — add the rest When 11 minutes remain on the timer, put the chicken ribs, breasts and wings into the deep fryer.
0:00
Timer ends — take everything out Remove all remaining chicken pieces once the timeout is reached. Ready to serve.
Chicken tenders: repeat the same staggered process using a 3-minute timer.
Method B

All-In-One Cooking (Alternative)

When everything goes in together

Use this only when all regular chicken pieces are put into the deep fryer at the same time.

13:00
All pieces in — timer starts Every piece of regular chicken goes into the fryer together.
2:00
Timer reads 2:00 — pull the ribs, wings and breasts That is 11 minutes of frying time elapsed out of the 13-minute total. Take out the ribs, wings and breasts.
0:00
Timer ends — thighs and drumsticks out Leave the thighs and drumsticks in to finish cooking until the timeout is reached.
Watch the clock carefully: at 2:00 remaining on the timer, the ribs, wings and breasts must come out. Only the thighs and drumsticks stay in until 0:00.

4. Completion

Finish the batch cleanly and hand it over for service.

Step 04

Out of the Fryer, Onto the Counter

Ready to serve
Wait for the timeout. Take all remaining chicken pieces out of the fryer once the timeout is reached — not before.
Confirm the batch is complete. Every piece that went in comes out. Nothing is left in the oil.
Ready to serve customers. Hand the batch over to the serving counter.

Safety & Quality Reminders

  • Never overfill the fryer — keep the oil 1 cm below the max line.
  • Only add chicken once the oil is at 180°C and the "small fire" indicator is showing.
  • Do not leave the fryer unattended while chicken is cooking.
  • Keep the timer running for every batch — no estimating by eye.
  • Discard any chicken that is not fully defrosted or does not smell fresh.

Quick Reference

1
Oil & heat 17 litres of oil, 1 cm below max line, set to 180°C.
2
Breading Flour → water → flour. Regular 20 pcs, tenders 3 pcs.
3
Method A — staggered Thighs & drumsticks at 13:00. Ribs, breasts & wings at 11:00. All out at 0:00.
4
Method B — all-in Everything in at 13:00. Ribs, wings & breasts out at 2:00. Thighs & drumsticks out at 0:00.
5
Tenders 3-minute timer, same breading and frying steps.
6
Completion All pieces out at timeout. Ready to serve.

End of Procedure
